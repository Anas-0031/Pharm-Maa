What are GLP-1 medicines?

GLP-1 (Glucagon-Like Peptide-1) receptor agonists are a class of prescription medicines that help regulate blood sugar and appetite. Originally developed for type 2 diabetes, they have become breakthrough treatments for weight management. They work by mimicking a natural hormone that slows digestion, reduces hunger, and improves insulin response. These are prescription-only medicines and should only be taken under medical supervision.
